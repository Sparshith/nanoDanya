KV Cache doesn't matter here

nanoDanya chess model inference benchmark
KV cache delivers a 4.12x speedup on CPU, but on the GPU that actually serves the model, the gain is a negligible 1.11x. With sequences of only ~80 tokens, the T4 is so underutilized that kernel launch overhead dominates over the compute saved by caching.
