# GRPO v1: Winprob Reward on Mixed Positions Keeps Strength, Keeps Most Puzzle Gains

Date: 2026-08-26.

## Setup

v1 implements the two fixes from the v0 verdict: dense eval reward instead of
binary first-move, and mixed positions so the reward and the KL leash cover
ordinary play, not only puzzles.

- Data: `datasets/mixed/grpo-eval` (`data_prep/prepare_grpo_eval_positions.py`),
  120k positions, 117,548 train / 2,452 val by game-id hash, first 100k games
  excluded. 50% ordinary game plies (random ply >= 16), 50% puzzle positions
  (post lead-in). Each record: token prefix + SAN -> winprob table over every
  legal move from one fairy-stockfish MultiPV search (movetime 0.2s, avg depth
  10.6, avg 30.7 legal, zero multipv misses). Winprob = 1/(1+exp(-0.00368208*cp)),
  cp clamped +/-1000, mover's perspective. Quality: winprob spread 0.63/position;
  played move ranks top-1 69.8%, avg rank 2.74.
- Trainer: `training/train_grpo.py` rewritten in place (v0 in git history).
  Reward = sampled move's winprob (all annotation variants); illegal draws get
  ILLEGAL_REWARD=0.0, below the 0.025 clamp floor. Group-mean baseline cancels
  the per-position offset, so the gradient equals eval-delta. Same GRPO core:
  K=8 at temp 0.8, kl_beta 0.05, lr 1e-5, effective batch 64 (micro 16 x
  grad-accum 4; micro 64 OOMs on A10G, mixed prefixes run 300-500 tokens).
- Val fix that matters: VAL_BATCHES defaults to 1 (= one micro-batch of 16
  positions per eval, pure noise). Pilot ran EVAL_INTERVAL=25, VAL_BATCHES=64
  (1,024 positions/eval). v0's dial readings were also thin; treat them as such.
- Checkpoint benchmarked: `checkpoints/grpo/eval-v1/grpo_v1_pilot_best.pt`
  (step 275 of 300).

Pilot dials (1 -> 300): expected winprob 0.524 -> 0.568, gap-to-best 0.167 ->
0.126, p(best) 0.236 -> 0.250, illegal mass 1.9% -> 1.4% (down, v0 flat), KL ~0.20
plateau (v0 0.5-0.65), entropy 1.05 -> 0.74. Still improving at step 300, unlike
v0's 75-step saturation. Learner-facing dial charts: plot/grpo-v1-pilot-dials/.

## Reading

- The v0 failure is fixed. v0 traded 24.5 points of SF1500 score for its puzzle
  gains; v1 keeps ~80% of those gains (+8.7 first-move, +7.9 full-solve over
  champion) at unchanged strength (SF1500 within noise, H2H 53.0% slightly
  positive, legality 0.27% vs 0.20% within noise).
- Strength is preserved, not yet improved: SF1500 49.75% vs 53.25% and H2H 53.0%
  bracket zero. The two strength reads mildly disagree; both are ~1 sigma events.
  Call it a tie.
- Mechanism consistent with design: KL plateau at 0.20 (vs v0 0.5-0.65) with the
  leash and reward now covering ordinary positions; illegal mass fell during
  training instead of regressing 3x on benchmark.
- The curve was still rising at step 300 with no dial degrading; the pilot
  undertrained if anything.

## Verdict

Machinery + reward are now both right: no strength tax, real tactics gains.
Champion unchanged for now (promotion needs SF1500 clearly above 53.25%, H2H
clearly above 50%). Next levers: longer run (1000+ steps, early stop on the
1,024-position expected-winprob dial) to see if strength follows the dials up;
drift-anatomy probes (kl_by_distribution, disagreement_quality) on the v1
checkpoint to confirm middlegame disagreements now net positive cp.

## Extension (2026-08-29): resumed to early stop, ceiling mapped

Pilot best (275) resumed with optimizer state as `grpo_v1_long.pt`, MAX_ITERS
1500, early stop on neg_reward (patience 10 evals / min_delta 5e-4 / min_steps
500), plus a new `dead_groups` val metric (fraction of K-draw groups with zero
reward spread). Early-stopped at 875; best step 625, expected winprob 0.5733
(pilot 0.568). Curve: slow grind to 625, then 250 flat steps with KL creeping
0.20 -> 0.27 and dead_groups 37% -> 47% (policy sharpening = groups collapse to
one move = idle compute).

Verdict suite on `grpo_v1_long_best.pt`, same protocol (`*_grpo_v1_long_*`):
first-move 63.3% / full-solve 45.8% (pilot 63.6/46.0), real-illegal 0.20%
(= champion), SF1500 52.1% (pilot 49.75, champion 53.25), H2H vs champion
50.1% (pilot 53.0). The extension recovered legality and most of the SF1500
gap; puzzles unchanged. Final position of the v1 line: champion strength +
~8 points puzzle tactics + champion legality. No promotion. The single-position
winprob recipe on human-game positions is harvested; next lever is position
distribution (self-play positions, winning/late-middlegame oversampled,
targeting the conversion cliff from the 2026-08-28 bot-games analysis).
